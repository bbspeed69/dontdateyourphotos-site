What the app does

The app helps you review photos and videos in your own device's photo library and decide which to keep and which to delete. It runs entirely on your device.

Deleting photos — read this one

You choose what gets deleted. Nothing is removed automatically, and nothing is removed until you confirm it on a screen that tells you exactly what will happen.

When you confirm, iOS moves those photos to the Recently Deleted album, where they remain recoverable for 30 days from within the Photos app. After that, iOS removes them permanently. We cannot recover a deleted photo for you at any point — we never have a copy of it, and no such copy exists anywhere outside your device.

You are responsible for the deletions you confirm. If your photos matter to you, keep a backup — iCloud Photos, a computer, or anywhere else — before deleting in bulk. This is good advice regardless of which app you use.

Free use and paid plans

The app is free to download and includes a number of free decisions. Beyond that, continued unlimited use requires a paid plan:

  • Keep it casual — an auto-renewing weekly subscription. It renews automatically each week until you cancel, and your Apple ID is charged at the start of each period.
  • Commit — a one-time purchase. It does not renew and there is nothing to cancel.

Prices are shown in the app in your local currency before you buy. If you decline to buy, the app keeps working with a weekly allowance of free decisions.

Billing, renewal and cancellation

All purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. We never see or handle your payment details.

A weekly subscription renews automatically unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. Manage or cancel it in Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions on your iPhone. Cancelling stops future renewals; the plan stays active until the end of the period you've already paid for.

Refunds are handled by Apple under their own policies, not by us. You can request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.
