Don't date your photos

You're in a toxic relationship with your camera roll.

Thousands of them. Most you'll never look at again. All of them still taking up space in your life.

Swipe left to break up. Swipe right to keep them forever. Swipe up for the ones you're still in love with. Nothing is deleted until you review it and say so.

It never asks twice

Every decision is remembered. Once you've dealt with a photo, it's gone from the deck for good — so you can put the phone down mid-session and pick it up a week later without starting over.

Everything stays on your phone

No account. No sign-in. No analytics. Your photos are read to show them to you and nothing else — they're never copied, never uploaded, never seen by anyone but you. Read the full privacy policy if you'd like the specifics.

Deleting is reversible

Photos you break up with go to the iOS Recently Deleted album, where they stay recoverable for 30 days. If you change your mind, they're right there in the Photos app.
