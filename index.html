GOOD TO KNOW

No mixed signals.

What happens to photos I delete?+

First, you review the photos you’ve marked. After you confirm deletion, iOS normally moves them to Recently Deleted, where they may be recoverable for up to 30 days unless permanently deleted sooner. Check the Photos app for available recovery options.

Does the app collect my photos?+

No. We don’t receive your photo library or sorting history. If you choose to share a photo, iOS sends it to your chosen destination. Software updates use technical identifiers and diagnostics. Our privacy policy explains the details.

How much does it cost?+

You can try the app for free. For more sorting, explore the available purchase options in the app. Apple shows your local price and terms before you buy.

Subscriptions renew automatically unless canceled. Manage or cancel them in your Apple subscription settings. See our terms for more information.

Photo permissions, purchases, or a question about sorting? Visit Support and we’ll help you find your next step.
